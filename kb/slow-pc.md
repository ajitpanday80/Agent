## Steps
1. Save work, close programs and browser tabs that are not needed, and restart the computer.
2. If still slow, ask whether the drive is nearly full. Storage Sense in Settings under System, Storage can clear temporary files.
3. Ask whether it is slow at startup. Too many startup apps can cause that; a technician can review them.

## Escalate if
Still slow after a restart, slow for several people, or any sign of malware such as pop-ups or unknown programs. Treat sudden slowness plus strange behavior as a possible security issue.
