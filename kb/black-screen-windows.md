## Steps
1. Ask whether the computer seems to be on: lights on, fans running, any sound. Ask if it is a laptop or a desktop with a separate monitor.
2. Check power. For a laptop, ask them to plug in the charger. For a monitor, ask whether its power light is on, and that its cables are firmly in at both ends.
3. For an external monitor, ask them to check it is on the right input and to try pressing the Windows key and P together, then choose another display mode.
4. Ask them to press the Windows key, Control, Shift and B together. A beep or flicker means Windows reset the display.
5. If the screen is black but there is a mouse pointer, ask them to press Control, Shift and Escape to open Task Manager.
6. As a last step, ask them to hold the power button for about twenty seconds until it turns off, wait, and then turn it on again.

## Escalate if
Still black after the power-off and restart; a blinking cursor stays on screen (the computer may not find its startup drive); an error message appears; or the monitor works on another computer but not this one. Do not ask the caller to use Safe Mode or Recovery by phone.

## Technician notes
From Microsoft's guide: boot to Windows Recovery by interrupting startup two or three times, then Troubleshoot, Advanced options, Startup Settings, and choose Safe Mode with Networking; in Device Manager update or roll back the display adapter driver; use System Restore to a point before the problem began; a blinking cursor can mean the firmware cannot find a boot device; blank screens often follow incompatible drivers or incomplete updates.
