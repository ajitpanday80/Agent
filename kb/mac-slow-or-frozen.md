## Steps
1. If one app is frozen, ask the caller to try quitting it normally with Command and Q. A normal quit lets the app save changes.
2. If it will not quit, ask them to press Option, Command and Escape together, choose the frozen app, and click Force Quit. Warn them that unsaved changes in that app may be lost.
3. If the whole Mac is slow, ask them to quit apps and browser tabs they are not using, then restart the Mac from the Apple menu.
4. Ask whether the Mac is nearly out of storage, since a very full startup disk slows it down. A technician can help free space.
5. If the Mac will not restart, ask them to hold the power button for up to ten seconds until it turns off, then press it again to start.

## Escalate if
Still slow after a restart, slow for several people, or any pop-ups or unknown programs appear. Treat sudden slowness together with strange behavior as a possible security issue.

## Technician notes
From Apple's guide: check Activity Monitor for memory use and apps needing more memory than available; check free disk space on the startup disk.
