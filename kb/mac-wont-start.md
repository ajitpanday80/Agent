## Steps
1. Ask what happens when they press the power button: no light or sound at all, an Apple logo that stops, or a progress bar that never finishes.
2. Check power. Ask them to confirm the cable and outlet work and, for a laptop, to leave it plugged in for a while to charge. Ask them to try another charger or outlet if they have one.
3. Ask them to disconnect everything: printers, drives, hubs, phones and other accessories, and try again.
4. If the screen is dark, ask them to check the brightness or, for an external display, that it is on and connected.
5. Ask them to hold the power button for about ten seconds until the Mac turns off, wait a few seconds, then press it normally.

## Escalate if
It still will not start, or it starts and stops again. Do not ask the caller to reset anything or reinstall the system by phone. If the Mac only works in Recovery, the system on the startup disk may be damaged, and a technician needs to decide between reinstalling and restoring from backup.

## Technician notes
From Apple's guides: reset the SMC only on Intel Macs, which does not apply to Apple silicon; if the Mac turns on but does not finish starting, try startup safe mode, resetting NVRAM, or macOS Recovery; recently installed memory or drives must be compatible and correctly seated; if Recovery works but normal startup does not, reinstall macOS or restore from a backup; otherwise contact Apple Support or an authorized service provider.
